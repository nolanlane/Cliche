# 1. Executive summary

**Final verdict: PRE-ALPHA.** Cliche is a runnable Godot 4.7.2 first-person horror prototype with seven authored sectors, a finite lighter, and a stalking Observer.
The editor scan and geometry reachability audit pass, but the documented controller walkthrough currently stops at 5/24 targets and the Observer route audit fails at the sump (see MAJOR #3 and #4).
The only verified end state is player defeat and restart; no player objective or successful ending was found (BLOCKER #1), and packaged export is unverified (BLOCKER #2).
The current horror experience is a short exploration-and-evasion loop, not a complete investigation or survival campaign (BLOCKER #1; MAJOR #6-7).
Recent commits show real feature work, but the checkout at audit start also contained 13 modified tracked files and 44 untracked files beyond `origin/main`.
Estimated progress toward a shippable standalone horror game: **30%**.

# 2. Project state map

**Engine and entry points.** This is a Godot 4.7 project; the available engine is Godot 4.7.2. `project.godot:11-19` sets `res://main.tscn` as the main scene. `main.tscn:3-16,28-62` binds the level and gameplay scenes; `main.gd` owns the director and `player.gd` owns movement, the lighter, and player feedback.

**Structure.** `scenes/sectors/` contains seven level sectors plus the shared shell. `scenes/gameplay/` contains player, Observer, and HUD scenes. Reusable fixtures and pickups are in `scenes/components/`; gameplay scripts are in `scripts/`; materials and shaders are separate. `assets/observer/observer.glb` is the creature model. `tests/` contains geometry, traversal, Observer behavior, pose, and visual scripts. `artifacts/` contains screenshots and generated audit reports. `tools/` contains the Blender creature build script/source. `addons/godot-mcp/` is editor/runtime tooling.

**Build and run.** `godot --headless --path . --editor --quit` completed successfully on Godot 4.7.2. The project also launched in the editor and rendered its live first-person scene on the available Intel Iris Xe. The README tells developers to open the project and press Play (`README.md:17`). No `export_presets.cfg`, release build script, CI workflow, or target-platform instructions were found. A distributable export is therefore **UNVERIFIED**, not proven impossible (see BLOCKER #2).

**Documentation.** `README.md` lists controls and two level-validation commands (`README.md:5-30`). `docs/observer.md` describes the threat and test commands. There is no player-facing objective/ending guide, export guide, settings guide, or documented support matrix; see BLOCKER #1, BLOCKER #2, and MAJOR #6. The validation commands execute, but the controller walkthrough fails and contradicts its saved verification artifacts (see MAJOR #3).

**Git history and velocity.** Before this review commit, there were five commits total, all dated Sep 26–27, 2026; the history is a two-day burst, not a long maintenance record. The changes are substantive: `961aaf2` bootstraps the first-person project and assets (+5,995 lines); `a58362a` builds stalking/pursuit behavior (+936/-204); `e05eed0` adds the creature silhouette and capture sequence (+352/-95); `0d60492` adds the environmental director, lighting, and generated sound (+661/-105); `53e0241` moves the level into editable sector scenes (+11,193/-3,023 across 57 files). This is real feature and content work, though the final refactor is large. At audit start, `HEAD` and `origin/main` were both `53e0241`; the working tree differed by 13 tracked files (+16,235/-5,221) and 44 untracked files. The requested commit stages that existing work together with this review.

**Validation run for this review.** The geometry audit passed: 39,393 reachable floor cells, 89 unique fixtures, 37 gameplay targets, and all sectors/pickups/anchors reachable. The controller walkthrough failed at 5/24 stops, stalling at the East office-to-crawlspace approach after 66.1 simulated seconds (see MAJOR #3). The Observer behavior audit failed both sump-route checks and left the creature 28.16 m from its target (see MAJOR #4). The Observer pose audit passed; maximum measured planted-foot slide was 0.000596 m per frame. A fresh live-game log showed scene binding succeeded and no startup script errors; performance and export behavior were not profiled (see MINOR #11 and BLOCKER #2).

# 3. What it does well

- The main scene is a genuine playable 3D level, not a menu mock-up. `main.tscn:5-16,28-62` binds seven saved sector scenes, the player, Observer, HUD, and ambient stream. On launch, the director reported 89 troffers, 27 Observer anchors, 7 spatial emitters, and 10 fuel cans (`main.gd:267-275`; observed in the live log).
- Authored geometry has measurable reachability coverage. `tests/level_geometry_audit.gd:37-80,103-124` checks player-sized capsules, low clearance, sectors, pickups, anchors, and fixture duplication; the current run passed with 39,393 reachable cells. This is useful level evidence, though it does not replace the failed real-controller walkthrough (see MAJOR #3).
- The Observer has a testable counterplay loop: it tracks sight and sprint noise, pursues the last known position, requires physical contact and a clear path to capture, and disengages after the player breaks contact (`main.gd:726-752`). The current behavior audit passed the 40-point reception route, low doorway, crawlspace refuge, and quiet escape checks (`tests/observer_behavior_audit.gd:55-76,113-123`), but failed at the sump (see MAJOR #4).
- The imported creature's gait has a numerical pose check. `tests/observer_pose_audit.gd:15-46` covers idle, creep, stalk, and run speeds; this run passed with planted-foot slide below 0.000596 m/frame and ankle heights between 0.111 m and 0.298 m.
- The game starts and renders on the available machine, and the level's sectors are spatially distinct in the current contact sheet (`artifacts/level-layout/contact-sheet.jpg`). The archive shelves, pillar forest, crawl opening, sump, and narrowing terminal hall provide more layout variation than a single rectangular room.

# 4. What needs genuine work

## BLOCKER

1. **There is no player-facing completion loop.** The README explains movement, lighter use, and refills, but no goal (`README.md:5-17`). The only explicit terminal flow found is the defeat overlay and `R` scene reload (`main.gd:1194-1230`); a repository search found no victory, ending, or extraction implementation. **Fix:** define an objective, communicate progress in play, and implement and test a successful ending. Until then, this is a walk-and-evade prototype, not a complete game.
2. **A shippable build is unverified.** The Godot project imports and runs, but `project.godot:11-31` defines the game project/main scene and development tooling; no `export_presets.cfg` or release build workflow was found in the repository. The README stops at editor Play (`README.md:17`). **Fix:** choose a supported platform, add an export preset/build command, export a clean build, and test that exported build rather than treating editor launch as release validation.

## MAJOR

3. **The documented controller walkthrough currently fails, and its pass report is stale.** The fresh README command reached 5/24 targets, then reported `Crawl entry: Stalled at (13.81089, 0.000839, -20.40002) toward (13.6, 0.015, -20.4)`; it also left six fuel cans uncollected. The real-controller route and stall detector are in `tests/level_walkthrough.gd:51-75,113-161`. In contrast, `artifacts/level-layout/verification.md:7-13` and `artifacts/level-layout/walkthrough.json:1-32` claim 24/24 stops and 340.5 seconds. **Fix:** reproduce the door approach with the actual player capsule, correct either the collision/layout or route-following test, rerun from a clean state, and replace the pass artifacts only after the same commands pass.
4. **The Observer cannot complete the audited route into the sump.** The fresh behavior audit reports an empty route from the reception endpoint to the sump and a final distance of 28.1579 m (`tests/observer_behavior_audit.gd:78-88`; current result in `artifacts/observer-overhaul/behavior-audit.json:1-33`). The navigation grid samples at 0.65 m and links neighbors only when the height difference is at most 0.48 m (`scripts/observer_navigation.gd:29-82`). **Fix:** make ramp traversal continuous in the collision-derived graph or add validated ramp waypoints, then require a complete route and arrival in the behavior audit.
5. **A first-time player can be caught before getting oriented.** The game begins directly in play; the Observer's initial delay is 7-10 seconds (`main.gd:255-264`). In six deterministic behavior trials, first sight was 7.8 seconds and an idle player was caught in 16.7-17.7 seconds (`tests/observer_behavior_audit.gd:32-50`; observed this run). **Fix:** add a protected intro/onboarding state and start the first encounter after the player has moved or finished the tutorial, while preserving the later slow-burn pacing.
6. **There is no pause/options/save layer or controller mapping.** The input map defines keyboard and mouse actions only (`project.godot:33-76`); `scenes/gameplay/hud.tscn` contains a status card and vignette, not a menu. A repository search of `main.gd`, `player.gd`, `scenes/`, and `project.godot` found no save/load, pause, settings, or gamepad implementation. **Fix:** add pause/resume, mouse sensitivity, FOV and audio controls, controller actions, and persisted preferences; decide whether a checkpoint is required for the intended run length.
7. **The audio implementation is still a procedural placeholder for a genre where sound carries the fear.** No WAV/OGG/MP3/FLAC source assets were found. The hum, sector beds, lighter effects, footsteps, breathing, and creature footfalls are synthesized from oscillators/noise in `main.gd:455-523`, `player.gd:468-573`, and `scripts/observer_audio.gd:15-53`. I did not monitor the live mix, so its perceived quality is **UNVERIFIED**. **Fix:** author or license distinct electrical, room-tone, movement, and threat sounds; mix and listen on headphones and speakers; ensure sound cues teach distance and danger rather than merely fill silence.

## MINOR

8. **The visual language repeats the same surface treatment across most of the map.** The contact sheet shows useful sector geometry, but most rooms reuse the yellow wallpaper, brown carpet, and suspended tile ceiling (`artifacts/level-layout/contact-sheet.jpg`; `materials/wallpaper.tres:1-11`, `materials/floor_carpet.tres:1-15`). **Fix:** keep the Backrooms identity but give key sectors a small number of distinct material, lighting, prop, and story-clue variations so navigation and escalation read without adding arrows.
9. **The HUD and defeat screen are hard to read at a glance.** Control instructions are 10 px and muted and omit mouse-look/Esc guidance shown in the README (`scenes/gameplay/hud.tscn:53-61`; `README.md:5-13`); the defeat overlay uses low-contrast text over black (`main.gd:1209-1219`; `artifacts/observer-overhaul/caught.png`). **Fix:** increase contrast and scalable text, surface the missing controls, and verify readability at common window sizes.
10. **The Godot MCP runtime/editor tools are enabled in the normal project configuration.** `project.godot:17-31` autoloads the runtime bridge and enables the editor plugin; a normal game run opened the localhost runtime listener. **Fix:** gate both behind a development-only feature or exclude them from release exports.
11. **Performance is UNVERIFIED.** No profiler report or target-spec measurements were found in the repository. The director updates every fixture (`main.gd:277-305`) while navigation builds a 21,297-cell collision-derived graph (`scripts/observer_navigation.gd:29-51`). **Fix:** profile startup, frame time, and memory in the export on minimum target hardware, especially during initial navigation baking and an active chase.

# 5. Horror-quality gap analysis

- **Atmosphere and visual storytelling vs. Outlast and Amnesia: The Dark Descent — MINOR gap.** The yellow liminal identity is clear, and the contact sheet shows varied layouts, but the rooms rely primarily on the same material and lighting vocabulary; the checked-in art contains no verified narrative clues or scripted story beats. See `materials/wallpaper.tres:1-11`, `materials/floor_carpet.tres:1-15`, and `artifacts/level-layout/contact-sheet.jpg`. **Fix:** add a few deliberate environmental story reveals and distinct sector signatures rather than more generic dressing. Outlast's authored set pieces and Amnesia's recognizable spaces set a much higher bar for memorable room-to-room escalation.
- **Stalker behavior vs. Amnesia: The Bunker — MAJOR gap.** Cliche has actual sight/noise response and fair physical capture checks (`main.gd:726-752`), plus a passing multi-corner reception route. However, the fresh audit cannot route the Observer to the sump and confirms the crawl duct is the only tested refuge (`tests/observer_behavior_audit.gd:78-88,122-123`). **Fix:** repair cross-sector route reliability and add more deliberate search/hide choices before tuning chase speed. The current implementation is a promising single-enemy loop, not yet a reliable systemic stalker.
- **Chase and resource rhythm vs. Outlast — MAJOR gap.** Sprinting is both escape and noise; lighter fuel drains at 1.18 units/second (about 85 seconds) and refills at pickups (`player.gd:48-51,715-739,780-789`). The lighter currently supplies light, but there is no camera-like information tool or objective pressure tied to its use. **Fix:** make fuel decisions change what the player can learn or safely do, and validate each refill route with the corrected walkthrough.
- **Tension audio vs. Iron Lung — MAJOR gap.** Cliche has a continuous generated hum, sector-specific noise beds, thumps, silence, and positional synthesized Observer audio (`main.gd:437-523`, `scripts/observer_audio.gd:1-53`). It has no player-operated audio instrument or verified sound-led navigation clues; without recorded source material and a reviewed mix, the current soundscape reads as a prototype implementation. **Fix:** make audio convey spatial information and encounter timing, then mix and test the finished cues.
- **Investigation loop vs. Phasmophobia — BLOCKER for a complete game.** The verified loop is explore, collect lighter fuel, and evade one pursuer (`README.md:5-17`; `project.godot:33-76`). No evidence collection, case objective, multiplayer, ghost-identification, or win condition was found. **Fix:** define the investigation task and success criteria, then add the minimum tools and evidence interactions needed to make each run a decision-making loop rather than open-ended roaming.
- **Pacing and onboarding vs. Amnesia and Outlast — MAJOR gap.** The tested first sighting at 7.8 seconds and idle capture by 17.7 seconds leave almost no safe time to absorb controls in a game with no start/menu screen (`main.gd:255-264`; `tests/observer_behavior_audit.gd:32-50`). **Fix:** protect the opening, then pace later encounters around player progress and authored quiet intervals instead of relying only on timers.

# 7. Progress assessment

**Estimated completion: 30% toward release.** The project has a working engine entry point, a seven-sector authored map, movement and lighter systems, and an implemented stalking creature with several tested counterplay behaviors. Those are meaningful prototype milestones. Release-critical work remains: no player goal or win state, the current documented traversal and Observer sump audits fail, there is no export pipeline or user settings layer, and the audio/visual presentation lacks the authored content and validation expected of a finished horror game. This percentage is a reviewer estimate of release-feature completeness, not a count of files or lines; it reflects the blockers and audit failures in BLOCKER #1-2 and MAJOR #3-7, plus the presentation and validation gaps in MINOR #8-11.
